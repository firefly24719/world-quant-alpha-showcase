# Research governance

The research ledger keeps experiments and their actual settings, observation times and checks as dated evidence. Current account status is a separate snapshot. When ACTIVE membership changes, prior submission checks are historical observations and remaining candidates require a fresh check.

Research review considers discovery efficiency, diversity, annual stability and candidate conversion. Results guide the next search rather than being reduced to a Sharpe ranking. Failed or ambiguous runs remain visible in the research record.

Credentials, raw API payloads and daily PnL stay in private storage. This portfolio publishes aggregate evidence and explanatory case studies. Concrete expressions, Alpha IDs and account-linked records are not included.
