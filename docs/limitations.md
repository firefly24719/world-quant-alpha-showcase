# Limitations

- Account and eligibility counts are snapshots and can change after submission or platform updates.
- Candidate expressions and static passes are not independent discoveries or realized investment performance.
- Local pairwise daily-PnL correlation is a proxy for later official Self-Correlation checks.
- Historical 2019–2023 results were used in research. They do not establish fresh out-of-sample performance.
- The available evidence does not establish stock-level causal attribution, full point-in-time data coverage or elimination of overfitting.
- This portfolio is a curated description. Private platform records are not published here. The certificate PDF is an unchanged copy authorized by its holder for this portfolio.
