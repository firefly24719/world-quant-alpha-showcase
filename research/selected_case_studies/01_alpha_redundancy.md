# When two passing ideas overlap

Two price-position signals were initially attractive in isolation. After one was submitted, the other returned an official self-correlation of **0.8608**, above the **0.7** cutoff, and failed the platform's improvement exception. Its earlier pass described a different submitted portfolio.

The observation changed the decision process. Checking candidates individually against today's ACTIVE set does not reveal every conflict that will arise after a later submission. The research system also examines candidate-to-candidate daily PnL relationships before planning a sequence.

This case establishes an observed eligibility change. It does not establish that the two expressions are economically identical or that the same outcome will recur under every future account state.
