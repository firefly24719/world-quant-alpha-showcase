# Testing a competing price explanation

A trading-activity-to-price signal showed promising in-sample behavior. Because its construction also depends on price, the research question was whether the observed behavior contained information beyond a price-related baseline.

A matched price-position control was simulated using the same platform settings. The original signal's annual Sharpe exceeded the control in 2021 and 2022, but was lower in 2019 and 2023. An exploratory return-spanning comparison trained on 2019–2022 produced a negative residual Sharpe in the already-inspected 2023 period.

The evidence weakened a claim of stable independent trading-activity contribution. It did not isolate stock-level exposures or prove a causal mechanism. This outcome illustrates why a promising ratio and a plausible economic story still require a competing control.
