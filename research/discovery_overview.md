# Discovery overview

The discovery process combines fundamental, analyst, option and price-volume hypotheses with an explicit exploration allocation. Candidate generation records the proposed mechanism and its settings. Static checks address obvious expression, field and duplication issues before a platform simulation is considered.

In one historical group of waves, **107 discovery candidates**, **33 field probes** and **6 controls** completed simulation. These categories serve different purposes: a field probe tests availability, while a control tests a research question. Counting all 146 as independent investment ideas would obscure that distinction.

A separate later local pool contained 140 expressions, 132 passing static checks. Its queue was stopped before completion. Passing a static check establishes neither predictive power nor submission eligibility.

Failure feedback guides the next batch. For example, a news-based candidate retained a positive annual profile but failed the sub-universe check. Two single-change controls also failed that check, weakening the case for further small grouping changes as the immediate remedy.
