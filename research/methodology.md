# Research methodology

Each research cycle begins with a financial hypothesis and a competing explanation. Field definitions, data availability, settings and missing-value behavior are reviewed before a candidate is tested. A candidate family can contain several expressions, but changing a lookback or denominator alone does not establish a new mechanism.

The platform supplies the simulation and official checks. The analysis then considers annual performance, turnover, sub-universe behavior and daily-return relationships. When a result is interesting, matched comparisons are preferred for investigating what changed and why.

The workflow distinguishes a dated official result from a local forecast. Candidate-to-candidate correlations help plan an order, but future platform eligibility depends on the ACTIVE set at the time of each check. See [dynamic correlation](selected_case_studies/02_dynamic_correlation.md).

The 2019–2023 data used in these examples informed research decisions. The 2023 period is not a fresh, untouched holdout.
